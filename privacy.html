Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Vornix Discord Bot ("the Bot", "we", "our", or "us") collects, processes, stores, and protects information when users interact with the Bot on Discord.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used by a Discord server, the Bot may process or store the following information:

  • Discord User IDs.
  • Discord Server (Guild) IDs.
  • Discord Channel IDs.
  • Member role information and role changes.
  • Server Booster status and related booster information.
  • Message content when required for anti-scam, anti-link, and moderation features.
  • Bot configuration, preferences, and command settings.
  • Technical logs and error information necessary for operating and maintaining the Bot.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by the Bot is used only for providing and maintaining its Discord functionality, including:

  • Managing server members and role-related features.
  • Detecting Server Booster status changes.
  • Assigning or removing booster-related roles.
  • Providing welcome and goodbye notifications.
  • Detecting suspicious messages, scam attempts, and potentially malicious links.
  • Supporting automated moderation and server security.
  • Managing music, voice rooms, and community features.
  • Troubleshooting errors and improving Bot reliability.

3. Message Content Processing

When Message Content access is enabled and available, the Bot may process message content to identify suspicious links, scam messages, phishing attempts, and content that violates server moderation rules.

Message content is processed for server security and moderation purposes only. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or training machine learning or artificial intelligence models.

Message content may be retained in moderation logs only when necessary for security, troubleshooting, or moderation records, according to the applicable retention practices.

4. Data Storage and Retention

The Bot may store necessary information on its operator-managed server infrastructure, including databases, configuration files, and operational logs.

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the relevant Bot features, maintain server configurations, meet security requirements, and resolve technical issues.

Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information and the feature involved. Information that is no longer necessary may be deleted.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information or message content to third parties.

Information is not shared with third parties for advertising or commercial profiling. Information may be processed by Discord as part of the Discord platform and its services.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect stored information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Access to the Bot's infrastructure and stored information is restricted to authorized operators. Data-at-rest encryption and other security controls are applied according to the actual infrastructure configuration.

7. User Rights and Data Deletion

Users may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with their Discord account.

To submit a request, contact the Bot developer or administrator through the official support channel or the contact information provided below.

Valid requests will be reviewed and processed within a reasonable timeframe, subject to applicable legal, security, and operational requirements.

8. User Choices

Server administrators can manage the availability of supported Bot features through the Bot's configuration options, where available.

Administrators may disable optional moderation or message-monitoring features where those controls are provided.

9. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Users must comply with Discord's applicable age requirements and Terms of Service.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Bot's functionality, data practices, or legal requirements change.

Updated versions will be published at this page with a revised Last Updated date.
